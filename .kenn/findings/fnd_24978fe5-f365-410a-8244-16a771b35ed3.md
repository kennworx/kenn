kenn does NOT consult .gitignore for ingestion, and `[workspace] excludes` reaches ONLY code-language discovery (driver/walk.rs -> Workspace::is_workspace_excluded). The content pipelines (markdown/text/css/html) own their own discovery by design D11 (markdown/discover.rs) and honor ONLY their per-language `[language.X].excludes` — NOT `[workspace] excludes`, despite that field's "workspace-wide" doc comment. So to keep a directory out of the index (e.g. a local `.claude/` config dir whose files are markdown), add its glob to `[language.markdown].excludes` (and any other content language that ingests it), not to `[workspace] excludes` or .gitignore. `[workspace] excludes` is effectively code-only unless/until content discovery is wired to also consult is_workspace_excluded.
