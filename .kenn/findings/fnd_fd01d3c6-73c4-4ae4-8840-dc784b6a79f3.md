Index formats generically; never vendor a third-party framework's vocabulary. A producer must not recognize, name, or special-case any framework's element names, attribute names, or namespace URIs — correctness would then depend on which tools the authors had heard of. Where a specific vocabulary's meaning is genuinely needed, the workspace supplies it as configuration and kenn ships only the mechanism. The conventional identity carriers (an 'id' or 'name' attribute) are properties of the format, not of any vocabulary, and are fair game.
