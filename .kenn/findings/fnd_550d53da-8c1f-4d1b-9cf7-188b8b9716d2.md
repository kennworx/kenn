One lookup, one trait, one store-backed implementation. The class-registry lookup is declared twice — once in css/usage.rs and once in html/classes.rs — with two store-backed impls doing one job. That is the drift to avoid, not a pattern to follow: when a second producer needs an existing registry, it reuses the trait. Same for extraction — a shared text-to-references module has exactly one implementation, whatever origin hands it the text.
