The atlas OKF bundle (kenn-indexer/src/atlas/okf.rs) follows the OKF v0.1 standard strictly: concept frontmatter carries ONLY standard keys (type/title/description/resource/tags) — never producer-extension kenn.* keys. Symbol dependencies and central symbols live in the concept BODY, not frontmatter (central as a Markdown table: Symbol | pub_id | path:start-end, so an agent can run kenn get <pub_id> straight from the atlas). Do NOT work around shell-hostile pub_ids (backticks, parens, spaces) by escaping them in the md generator — the id grammar is fixed at its source (the shell-safe-symbol-ids change); the renderer stays standard.
