Edge payload columns in the SQLite store (`field_op`, `import_kind`, `corr_*`, and the new `link_grade`/`link_relation`) are WRITE-ONLY today: the reader never reconstructs `EdgeProperties` payloads — traversal filters by `kind` only and returns endpoints. So new payload (markdown link grade/relation) persists on write but has no read path until a consumer adds one (`check_links`, task 7.1, is the intended first edge-payload reader). The flatten match lives in `flatten_edge_properties` (split out of `write_batch` to keep its CRAP/cyclomatic under the gate).
