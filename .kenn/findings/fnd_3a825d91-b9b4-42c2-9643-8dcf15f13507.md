Container kinds (namespace/module/package) must never form a package anchor or a central symbol in the atlas. A C# namespace is pkg=0 by design (it spans assemblies) and would otherwise path-fall-back into a bare-name shadow concept. Key the exclusion on the ingester-provided `kind`.
