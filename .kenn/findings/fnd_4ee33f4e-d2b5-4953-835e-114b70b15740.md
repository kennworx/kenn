The vector sidecar is generation-namespaced: <vectors_root>/<model>/<dim>/<quant>/<recipe>/ (embed/sidecar/generation.rs), with the pre-generation flat dirs <vectors_root>/{code,findings} still readable as a same-generation LEGACY fallback (load_reuse_map_with_legacy). Do NOT remove that fallback as a cleanup: committed pack-*.bin files live in the legacy dirs and are what lets a fresh clone search offline (embedding-producer spec); dropping it silently forces a full re-embed. The same committed-class invariant governs GC: gc_vector_cache must never evict a dir holding pack-*.bin files, nor the manifest-matched active generation (which protects the legacy dir exactly while it still serves the active generation).
