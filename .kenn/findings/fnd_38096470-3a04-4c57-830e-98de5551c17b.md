A 'just crap-ci' failure reporting HUNDREDS of new over-threshold functions at 'coverage: 0.0' spanning every crate is a broken coverage collection, not a regression — re-run before believing it or touching the baseline. Observed once: 250 'new' entries, 247 at exactly 0.0 coverage, across all 9 crates, from a diff touching 3 files, with ZERO test failures in the same run; a clean re-run with nothing else touching cargo passed unchanged. Diagnose by counting statuses and the coverage distribution in the log, not by reading the offender list — a real regression is a handful of functions in files you actually touched, carrying plausible non-zero coverage. Do NOT run 'just crap-baseline' to make this go away; that bakes real debt in (CLAUDE.md section 6).
