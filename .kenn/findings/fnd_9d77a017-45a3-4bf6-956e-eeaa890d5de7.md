Never truncate an atlas list silently. A capped list that says nothing reads as the WHOLE list: on a real 125-package solution one utility package showed 8 of its 100 dependents, indistinguishable from a package that genuinely has 8, and 27 of 91 packages were cut that way. render_couplings names the cap when it binds (`## Used by — 100 packages, heaviest 24`) and stays clean when it does not, so the suffix itself signals truncation. The caps are ASYMMETRIC by direction and must stay that way: outgoing dependencies are a design CHOICE a package makes and the heaviest 8 already reveal a package with too many, while incoming ones are a POPULARITY measure it does not control and legitimately reach into the hundreds (MAX_DEPENDENTS = 24 vs MAX_DEPS = 8). The producer therefore returns (rows, total) with total counted BEFORE the cap — a renderer cannot reconstruct what it never received. This applies to any bounded atlas list, not just coupling: state what was dropped.
