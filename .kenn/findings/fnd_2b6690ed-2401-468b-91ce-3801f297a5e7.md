FIXED (was fnd_7fcaaa59, deferred): the spurious zero-range def rows are now removed at store finalize. SqliteWriter::finalize runs 'DELETE FROM defs WHERE start_line=0 AND sym_id IN (SELECT sym_id FROM defs WHERE start_line>=1)' — dropping placeholder defs that shadow a real def, keeping placeholders for truly synthetic/defined-nowhere symbols so they stay addressable. Root cause: the per-document SCIP/JSONL transform (walk.rs / stream.rs) emits a [0,0,0,0] placeholder for every symbol in a document's symbol table lacking a LOCAL Definition occurrence, but such a symbol may be defined in another document; the store-finalize dedup is the cross-document aggregation point where this is decidable. ~82 rows eliminated on a full self-index. Verified by the finalize_drops_placeholder_defs_that_shadow_a_real_def unit test.
