All snapshot->Ready binding in kenn-mcp funnels through ONE implementation: open_binding (opens the reader + registers the GC pin + parses the snapshot's meta.json into ReadyParts) followed by ready_from_parts, both in crates/kenn-mcp/src/indexing/orchestrate.rs. Its consumers are ready_from_parts (cold start / recovery / self-publish reindex completion) and swap_to_snapshot (external live-flip, in-place). Do NOT hand-roll a second open-reader/register-pin/build-Ready sequence — open_ready_if_live (tools/state.rs) previously did and silently skipped the run-metadata parse that get_index_status needs; it now delegates to open_binding. Anything that must run at every snapshot bind (pins, meta parse, cache warming) belongs in open_binding, once. LifecycleState::Ready carries run_meta (boxed to keep the variant small); get_index_status reads it from cached state and does NO meta.json read on the tool call path.
