Embedding task prompts are applied exactly once, in kenn-embed's producer.rs task_prompt() — shared by the in-process (LlamaEmbedder) and remote (RemoteEmbedder, client-side) producers. The kenn-server /v1/embeddings handler MUST keep submitting EmbedKind::Document (raw pass-through): the OpenAI wire carries no query-vs-document field, clients pre-apply prompts before sending, so prompting server-side (e.g. keying off the X-Kenn-Priority header) would double-prompt every RemoteEmbedder query.
