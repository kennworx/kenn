The atlas is data-dependent, not code-dependent, and MUST NOT depend on kenn-analyze — the two are parallel consumers of the persisted graph. It reads the aggregate nodes+edges (central symbols = weighted degree) AND, for the domains axis, the persisted analysis tables (analysis_flat_communities + analysis_node_membership) read back on the WRITER's own connection via DbWriter::scan_analysis_* — it does NOT recompute clustering. So in compute_and_persist the analysis hook runs BEFORE the atlas (cloning nodes/edges), guaranteeing the flat-Louvain tables exist when the atlas reads them; with no atlas the hook takes the originals.
